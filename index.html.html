How We Work

A disciplined
search methodology.

Executive search for PE-backed companies demands more than a database. It demands a proprietary process calibrated to holding periods, investment theses, and board dynamics. Here's how we operate.

01

Sponsor & Management Alignment

We start by aligning with both the PE sponsor and management team on the ideal candidate profile, success metrics, and cultural fit. Misalignment here kills searches — we prevent it upfront.

02

Proprietary Market Mapping

We conduct a systematic mapping of the relevant talent universe — including passive, non-networked candidates who would never surface through referrals alone.

03

Structured Evaluation & Screening

Competency-based interviews, SaaS metric fluency assessments, and PE culture fit evaluation. We only advance candidates who can perform in a sponsor-backed environment.

04

Presentation & Selection

Curated slate of 3–4 finalists with detailed written assessments. We facilitate structured panel interviews and guide both sides through the decision process.

05

Reference Verification & Close

Deep reference checks, offer negotiation support, and a 90-day onboarding check-in. We stay involved until the executive is set up to succeed.

Our Perspective

The best consumer leaders are part merchant, part operator, part brand builder.

We assess consumer executives across three dimensions: commercial instinct, operational rigor, and the ability to build brands that sustain pricing power. In a PE-backed environment, those three qualities must coexist — and great candidates who excel at only one or two are rarely the right fit.

Our consumer practice draws on Nancy's deep network of retail and brand executives built over 27 years — and our shared fluency in the performance expectations of sponsor-backed businesses.

About the Founder

Nancy

Founder & CEO, Lumina Search Group

Emory University, B.A. 27+ Years in Executive Search

Built from
experience.

Nancy is a veteran talent executive with more than 27 years of experience at the intersection of high-growth technology and human capital. Over the course of her career, she has reviewed more than 4 million resumes, coordinated hundreds of thousands of interviews, and placed thousands of professionals across every level of an organization — from first hires to chief executives.

She founded Lumina Search Group to address a gap she observed repeatedly throughout her career: private equity and venture capital–backed software companies face uniquely high-stakes leadership decisions, yet are often underserved by generalist search firms that lack the technical fluency and PE ecosystem relationships to move with the speed and precision these environments demand.

Before founding Lumina, Nancy served as CEO of PathMatch, a digital career navigation platform that matched college students with modern career paths and connected employers with emerging talent at scale. Under her leadership, PathMatch developed proprietary matching technology that helped students identify in-demand career trajectories while enabling companies to build early-career talent pipelines and strengthen their employer brands with the next generation of professionals.

Prior to PathMatch, Nancy founded FILD, a technology-enabled recruiting firm that partnered with some of the most recognized names in tech — including Snap, Amazon, Warby Parker, and Netflix — to hire thousands of employees. FILD was acquired in 2018, a testament to the firm's operational excellence and the differentiated value of its approach.

Nancy holds a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology and Music from Emory University.
